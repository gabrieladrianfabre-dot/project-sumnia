  left=4pt,right=4pt,top=2pt,bottom=2pt,fontupper=\small,title=How to read this reviewer]
\textbf{Format assumed:} multiple choice (conceptual and applied), about 20\,s per question, all grades mixed.\\
\textbf{Card line:} \textbf{Q:} cue $\rightarrow$ \textbf{A:} \textit{answer} with a difficulty tag:
\qatag{E} easy, \qatag{A} average, \qatag{D} difficult.\\
\textbf{Orange boxes} list common confusions. These are the pairs most likely to show up as \emph{distractors} in multiple choice, so learn the distinguisher, not just the term.\\
\textbf{Items tagged}\textsuperscript{\,\textcolor{red}{\textbf{[VERIFY]}}} were uncertain or conflicting across sources. They are collected in \emph{Items to verify} at the end.\\
\textbf{Drill order:} grade sections first (cover the answer, say it aloud), then \emph{Rapid Fire}, then \emph{Cheat Sheets} the night before.
\end{tcolorbox}
