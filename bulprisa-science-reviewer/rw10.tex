\subsection{Real-World Problems (Grade 10)}
\probset{10}
{\small Read the situation, pick a letter within 20 seconds, then check the Answer Key.\par}
\begin{gradebody}

\textbf{Term 1}

\mcq{Vinegar and baking soda are mixed in a bottle, and a balloon on its mouth inflates. The evidence of a chemical reaction is}
{a colour change}{gas production}{light}{a precipitate}
{B}{\ce{CO2} gas forms from an acid--carbonate reaction.}{E}

\mcq{Which of these is a chemical change?}
{Candle wax melting}{Salt dissolving in water}{An iron gate rusting}{An ice cube melting}
{C}{Rust is a new substance (iron oxide).}{E}

\mcq{A yellow turmeric stain on a white shirt turns reddish-brown when washed with soap. The soap is}
{acidic}{basic}{a salt}{neutral}
{B}{Turmeric is a natural indicator that turns red-brown in bases.}{A}

\mcq{An antacid tablet relieves hyperacidity because it}
{coats the esophagus}{kills bacteria}{neutralizes excess hydrochloric acid}{adds more acid}
{C}{Base + acid $\rightarrow$ salt + water.}{E}

\mcq{An LPG stove burns with a sooty yellow flame instead of blue. This means}
{the stove is too cold}{the gas is very pure}{complete combustion}{incomplete combustion, which can produce CO and soot}
{D}{Too little oxygen gives incomplete combustion.}{D}

\mcq{Zinc in hydrochloric acid gives off a gas that makes a squeaky pop with a burning splint. The gas and reaction type are}
{\ce{H2}; single replacement}{\ce{Cl2}; combination}{\ce{CO2}; combustion}{\ce{O2}; decomposition}
{A}{\ce{Zn + 2HCl -> ZnCl2 + H2}.}{A}

\mcq{Silver nitrate solution is added to salt water and a white solid forms. The reaction type is}
{single replacement}{decomposition}{double replacement}{combination}
{C}{\ce{AgNO3 + NaCl -> AgCl v + NaNO3}; the partners swap.}{A}

\mcq{Limestone (\ce{CaCO3}) is heated in a kiln to make lime (\ce{CaO}) and \ce{CO2}. This is}
{decomposition}{double replacement}{neutralization}{combination}
{A}{One compound breaks into two simpler substances.}{A}

\mcq{In \ce{2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O}, \SI{4}{g} of hydrogen reacts completely with \SI{32}{g} of oxygen. The mass of water formed is}
{\SI{28}{g}}{\SI{4}{g}}{\SI{36}{g}}{\SI{32}{g}}
{C}{Mass is conserved: $4 + 32$.}{A}

\mcq{Burned paper seems to lose mass in open air. Why doesn't this break the law of conservation of mass?}
{Gases (\ce{CO2}, water vapour) escape into the air}{Mass is destroyed by fire}{Fire creates energy from mass}{The ash is lighter than atoms}
{A}{In a closed system the total mass stays the same.}{D}

\mcq{Chopped firewood burns faster than a whole log because it has}
{less oxygen}{higher density}{greater surface area}{more mass}
{C}{More exposed surface allows more collisions with oxygen.}{E}

\mcq{Fish vendors in the palengke cover fish with ice because the low temperature}
{kills all bacteria}{increases the fish's mass}{adds flavour}{slows the reactions that cause spoilage}
{D}{Lower temperature means fewer effective collisions and slower reactions.}{A}

\mcq{Hydrogen peroxide bubbles quickly on a wound because the enzyme catalase acts as a}
{indicator}{reactant}{product}{catalyst}
{D}{Enzymes speed up reactions without being used up.}{A}

\mcq{An instant cold pack gets cold when squeezed and shaken. The process is}
{neutralization}{endothermic}{combustion}{exothermic}
{B}{It absorbs heat from its surroundings.}{A}

\mcq{After you eat a big plate of rice, your blood glucose rises. Your body responds by releasing}
{oxytocin}{insulin}{adrenaline}{glucagon}
{B}{Insulin lowers blood sugar (negative feedback).}{A}

\mcq{On a hot April afternoon, your face turns red and you sweat. The redness is from}
{vasoconstriction}{high blood sugar}{shivering}{vasodilation that releases heat}
{D}{Wider skin vessels let more heat escape.}{A}

\mcq{During labour, contractions trigger more oxytocin, which strengthens the contractions further. This is}
{negative feedback}{no feedback}{positive feedback}{homeostatic balance}
{C}{The response amplifies the change until birth.}{D}

\mcq{A person with diabetes injects insulin because their}
{kidneys fail}{pancreas does not make enough insulin (or the cells don't respond)}{liver makes too much bile}{blood has too little glucose}
{B}{Without insulin, blood glucose stays too high.}{A}

\mcq{After years of spraying the same insecticide, mosquitoes in a barangay are no longer killed by it. Best explanation?}
{Mosquitoes learned to avoid it}{Mutations were caused on purpose}{The resistant mosquitoes survived and reproduced}{The insecticide got weaker}
{C}{This is natural selection of variants that already existed.}{A}

\mcq{Pythons have tiny hip and leg bones inside their bodies. These are}
{homologous to fins}{vestigial structures}{new adaptations}{analogous structures}
{B}{Leftover structures suggest that snakes evolved from legged ancestors.}{D}

\mcq{Many Philippine species, such as the tamaraw and the tarsier, live nowhere else. This is best explained by}
{isolation on islands leading to new species}{recent creation}{human introduction}{climate alone}
{A}{This is biogeographic evidence: isolation leads to speciation.}{A}

\mcq{``Giraffes got long necks by stretching to reach leaves, and passed this on to their young.'' Whose idea is this?}
{Mendel}{Darwin}{Wallace}{Lamarck}
{D}{Inheritance of acquired traits is Lamarck's idea, which is now rejected.}{E}

\textbf{Term 2}

\mcq{A fishpond can support only about 500 tilapia. Adding more causes slow growth and deaths. The number 500 is the pond's}
{carrying capacity}{population density}{birth rate}{biotic potential}
{A}{It is the maximum population the resources can sustain.}{E}

\mcq{Rabbits are released on an island with plenty of food and no predators. At first, the population grows}
{exponentially (J-curve)}{slowly and steadily}{not at all}{in a straight line downward}
{A}{Unlimited resources allow exponential growth.}{A}

\mcq{Making patis and bagoong is an example of}
{traditional biotechnology (fermentation)}{cloning}{modern biotechnology}{genetic engineering}
{A}{Microbes ferment the fish or shrimp with salt.}{E}

\mcq{Golden Rice was developed mainly to fight}
{iron deficiency}{rice pests}{drought}{vitamin A deficiency}
{D}{It produces beta-carotene, the precursor of vitamin A.}{A}

\mcq{Farmers who plant Bt corn usually need}
{more fertilizer}{more water}{no sunlight}{fewer insecticide sprays}
{D}{Bt corn makes its own protein that is toxic to corn borers.}{A}

\mcq{A common environmental concern about GM crops is}
{they need no soil}{possible gene flow to wild relatives}{they glow in the dark}{they cannot be eaten}
{B}{Transferred genes might spread and affect biodiversity.}{A}

\mcq{A car parked in the sun with its windows closed becomes very hot inside. This models}
{El Ni\~no}{acid rain}{the ozone hole}{the greenhouse effect}
{D}{Sunlight enters, and the heat is trapped.}{E}

\mcq{PAGASA declares an El Ni\~no. Farmers should prepare for}
{more earthquakes}{above-normal rainfall}{below-normal rainfall and drought}{colder nights only}
{C}{El Ni\~no usually brings dry conditions to the Philippines.}{A}

\mcq{Some coastal towns in Bulacan and Pampanga now flood at high tide even without rain. Two main causes are}
{sea-level rise and land subsidence from groundwater pumping}{Habagat and Amihan}{more typhoons and earthquakes}{mangrove growth and siltation}
{A}{The sea is rising while the land sinks.}{D}

\mcq{Replacing a coal plant with a geothermal plant is an example of climate change}
{neutralization}{mitigation}{adaptation}{acceleration}
{B}{It reduces emissions (mitigation); building sea walls would be adaptation.}{A}

\textbf{Term 3}

\mcq{GPS stations show parts of Luzon shifting a few centimetres each year. They are measuring}
{tides}{tectonic plate movement}{sea-level rise}{typhoon tracks}
{B}{GPS detects plate motion at the mm--cm level.}{E}

\mcq{Mayon and Bulusan lie in a line roughly parallel to the Philippine Trench because}
{the subducting plate melts and feeds a volcanic arc}{they formed at a divergent boundary}{they are hotspots}{rivers carved them}
{A}{Subduction produces a chain of volcanoes parallel to the trench.}{D}

\mcq{During a basketball shot, ignoring air resistance, the ball's horizontal velocity}
{increases}{becomes zero at the peak}{stays constant}{decreases}
{C}{No horizontal force acts on the ball.}{A}

\mcq{To kick a football as far as possible on level ground (ignoring air), kick at}
{\SI{45}{\degree}}{\SI{90}{\degree}}{\SI{60}{\degree}}{\SI{30}{\degree}}
{A}{\SI{45}{\degree} gives the maximum range.}{E}

\mcq{A rescue plane drops relief goods. The pilot releases them \emph{before} flying over the target because the goods}
{fall straight down}{keep the plane's horizontal velocity as they fall}{are pushed by the wind only}{slow down instantly}
{B}{A projectile keeps its horizontal motion while falling.}{D}

\mcq{Boxers ``roll with the punch'' by moving their head back. This reduces the force by}
{stopping the punch}{increasing the time of impact}{increasing momentum}{reducing the punch's mass}
{B}{The same change in momentum over a longer time means less force.}{A}

\mcq{A \SI{0.1}{kg} ball moves at \SI{20}{\meter\per\second}. Its momentum is}
{\SI{0.005}{\kilogram\meter\per\second}}{\SI{200}{\kilogram\meter\per\second}}{\SI{2}{\kilogram\meter\per\second}}{\SI{20.1}{\kilogram\meter\per\second}}
{C}{$p = mv = 0.1 \times 20$.}{E}

\mcq{A billiard ball hits an identical resting ball head-on and stops, while the second ball moves off at the same speed. The collision is}
{elastic}{explosive}{perfectly inelastic}{not a collision}
{A}{Momentum and KE are both fully transferred.}{D}

\mcq{Two jeepneys collide and lock together, moving as one. The collision is}
{explosive}{elastic}{frictionless}{perfectly inelastic}
{D}{Objects that stick together are perfectly inelastic.}{A}

\mcq{A \SI{1.5}{kW} aircon runs \SI{8}{h} a day for 30 days. Its energy use is}
{\SI{12}{kWh}}{\SI{45}{kWh}}{\SI{360}{kWh}}{\SI{240}{kWh}}
{C}{$1.5 \times 8 \times 30 = \SI{360}{kWh}$.}{D}

\mcq{The Philippines can produce a lot of geothermal power mainly because}
{it has many rivers}{it is near the equator}{it has strong winds}{it is volcanically active, along plate boundaries}
{D}{Magma heat near the surface creates geothermal reservoirs.}{A}
