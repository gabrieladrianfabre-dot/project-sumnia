\subsection*{Scope of fact-checking}
General science content (definitions, laws, classic experiments, and who proposed them) is standard junior-high textbook material and is not cited line by line. Only items that are Philippine-specific, recently changed, or count-based were checked online. Any such item that could not be confirmed is tagged \textcolor{red}{\textbf{[VERIFY]}}.
